01 Overview & Scope

Welcome to Techoverflow ("we," "us," or "our"), accessible at https://techoverflow.tech. Techoverflow provides digital subscription offerings, developer tools guidance, and smart home cloud-to-cloud integrations.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, retention, and disclosure of information when you access our website, interact with us via WhatsApp or email, or connect your account through Google Assistant, Google Home, or related Google developer actions.

By accessing our website or utilizing any of our integrations, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the practices detailed in this policy.

02 Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the minimum amount of information necessary to fulfill your orders, provide customer support, and operate connected integrations.

A. Information You Provide to Us Directly

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address (info@techoverflow.tech), phone number, and WhatsApp phone number/handle when you submit a request form or communicate with us.
  • Order & Service Inquiries: Details regarding the digital subscription, license, or guidance package you request.
  • Customer Support Correspondence: Feedback, questions, and notes provided during chat or email discussions.

B. Smart Home & Google Cloud-to-Cloud Integration Data

When you choose to link Techoverflow with Google Home, Google Assistant, or smart home ecosystems, we process:

  • Account Linking Identifiers: Secure OAuth authorization tokens and unique user identifiers provided by Google during account linking to authenticate your integration.
  • Device State & Attributes: Device IDs, device names, online/offline status, operational modes, and execution payloads (e.g., state change requests) required to synchronize device status with Google Home Graph.
  • Interaction & Command Logs: Ephemeral execution logs generated when you trigger commands via Google Assistant or the Google Home application.

C. Automatically Collected Technical Information

  • Server & Diagnostic Logs: Standard HTTP request data including IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and timestamps, used solely for system security, uptime monitoring, and DDoS defense.
  • Cookies: Techoverflow is a static website and does not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

03 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

  • Fulfilling Orders & Services: Processing subscription requests, confirming orders, and coordinating delivery via WhatsApp and email.
  • Operating Google Home Integrations: Authenticating account linking, routing smart home commands, and updating device states in the Google Home Graph.
  • Customer Service: Responding to inquiries, resolving technical issues, and providing onboarding guidance.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Guarding against unauthorized access, fraudulent requests, and malicious cyber activities.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Fulfilling our obligations under applicable laws and regulations.

04 Google Home & Cloud Integrations (Google API Compliance)

Google API & User Data Policy Compliance: Techoverflow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs, Google Home Developer Console, and Actions on Google to any other application will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the Google Home Developer Policies.

In accordance with Google developer standards:

  • We strictly access and process only the permissions, traits, and device states explicitly authorized by you during Google Home account linking.
  • We do not use data obtained through Google APIs or Google Home integrations to serve targeted advertising, conduct market research, or build behavioral user profiles.
  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties unless strictly necessary to provide or improve the core user-facing functionality of the smart home integration, comply with applicable law, or as part of a verified merger/acquisition.
  • Human employees do not read user command data unless you have given explicit consent for troubleshooting, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required to comply with law.

05 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to data brokers, advertisers, or third parties under any circumstances. We share information only with trusted service partners strictly for the operation of our platform:

  • Google Cloud & Google Home Platform: To exchange device telemetry, execute voice/app commands, and maintain Home Graph synchronization for linked accounts.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): To provide customer support, receive purchase requests, and communicate subscription credentials as initiated by you.
  • Hosting & Infrastructure Providers: Reputable cloud hosting providers that host our static web assets under strict confidentiality and security terms.
  • Legal Authorities: When compelled by valid legal process, court order, or applicable law, or to protect the vital security of our users and infrastructure.

06 Data Security & Storage

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

  • All communications with our website are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Account linking tokens and integration credentials are stored securely using industry-standard encryption protocols.
  • Access to administrative infrastructure is restricted to authorized personnel using multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or until you request its deletion.

07 Account Unlinking & Data Deletion Instructions

We give you complete control over your connected accounts and personal information. You can disconnect your integrations or request complete deletion of your data at any time.

How to Unlink Techoverflow from Google Home

You can disconnect Techoverflow from your Google Account at any time through the official Google Home app:

1
Open App

Open Google Home on Android or iOS

2
Settings

Tap Settings > Works with Google

3
Select

Locate Techoverflow in Linked services

4
Unlink

Tap Unlink Account & confirm

Upon unlinking, Google immediately revokes our access token and stops transmitting device states, telemetry, or commands to our integration servers.

Requesting Permanent Data Deletion

If you wish to permanently delete your contact records, order history, or integration logs from our systems, follow these simple steps:

  • Send an email to info@techoverflow.tech with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  • Include your name, the WhatsApp number or email address used when contacting us, and any Google Home user identifier (if applicable).
  • Alternatively, submit your request via our Contact Form.

Upon verification, we will permanently purge your personal information from our active databases and servers within 30 calendar days, subject only to records required for statutory tax or legal compliance.

08 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)), you may have the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data as described in Section 7.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Restrict or object to certain types of processing.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request that your data be transferred to you or another service provider in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@techoverflow.tech. We will respond promptly within the timeframe required by applicable law.

09 Children's Privacy

Our website, digital subscription services, and developer integrations are not designed for or directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately at info@techoverflow.tech so we can promptly delete the data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational changes, improvements to our services, or updates to Google developer platform requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."

We encourage you to periodically review this page to stay informed about how we safeguard your data.
